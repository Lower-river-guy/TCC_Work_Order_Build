Work Order Steps

  1. 1

    Start Here

    Enter the grade checker's initials in the Prefix box.

  2. 2

    Load Device

    Press the button to load and select the device name.

  3. 3

    Load Projects

    Press the button to load the available projects.

  4. 4

    Configure Work Order

    Choose the required project, template settings, and any other required options.

  5. 5

    Preview Work Orders

    Press Preview Work Orders and review the results.

  6. 6

    Push to WorksManager

    If the preview is correct, use Confirm Upload to push work orders to WorksManager.
